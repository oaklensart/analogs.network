GUIDE // THE RING

WHAT THIS IS

Every light on this ring is a real, independent website, run by one creative person. The ring is the directory: spin it, open a node, step through to the real site.

When someone new joins, a new socket lights up. The ring grows one light at a time.

READING THE RING

  • A dot is a member — a node. Its color is their discipline.
  • N#043Join order. Numbers are permanent and never reused — a low number means they were here early.
  • SThe big letter on a card is the first letter of the member’s own web address.
  • The traveling lights are signals riding the chain that links every member, in join order.

FINDING PEOPLE

INDEX / opens the directory. Type a name, a web address, a discipline — or a node number: 43, #43 and N#043 all find the same node.

Sort by RING (join order), A–Z, or FRESH PINGS (latest posts first). Click the same sort again to reverse it.

The colored chips filter by discipline — the list, the ring, and DRIFT all follow. DRIFT /// jumps somewhere unexpected.

THE CARD

OPEN SITE → goes straight to the member’s own site — a plain link. Nothing is tracked, nothing redirects.

SHARE hands you their address, clean.

Every focused node is also a link: bookmark or copy the page URL and it comes right back here.

JOIN // THE RING

This seat is holding for an invited member — lit before their yes. If it stays quiet the hold expires and the seat opens again; a number only becomes permanent when a member claims it.

Every light on this ring is a real, independent website run by one creative person. Yours can be one of them.

It’s free — always. No account, no subscription, nothing to install. Paid placement doesn’t exist here; that’s a founding rule of the network, not a policy that can quietly change.

No feeds, no ads, no algorithm. Just doors to real sites, and a ring that grows one light at a time.

The whole process is one email: send your site’s address to The Monitor and your seat lights up.
